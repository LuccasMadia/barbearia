## Direction contract

THESIS: A barbershop template that reads as a private members' club, not a generic "book now" landing page — the category default this refuses is the bright, cheerful, stock-photo SaaS-booking look. Confidence and craft stand in for marketing copy.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black charcoal ground (`#0b0b0c`–`#141414`) with a single warm brass/gold accent (`#c9a24b` family) carrying CTAs, active states, and hairline rules; a refined serif display (Fraunces) for headlines paired with a clean geometric sans (Inter) for body/UI; 1px gold hairlines and generous negative space replace boxed cards; a drawn scissors mark is the recurring signature motif, never emoji or a stock icon font.

STORY: A visitor decides in one glance this shop is run by someone serious about the craft, sees real services/prices without digging, and either books immediately from the hero or scrolls through a short, confident story (services, barbers, hours/location) that keeps reinforcing the same restraint.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed dark hero, barber-chair photographic treatment (desaturated, vignette) on the right two-thirds, left-aligned kicker-free serif headline + one-line subhead + single gold CTA on the left third; thin fixed header above it with wordmark + scissors mark, nav links, and a bordered "Agendar" button.

FORM: Brief-pinned via the user's reference screenshot (dark/gold barbershop template); no concept-seed roll — direction confirmed directly with the user before build.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
